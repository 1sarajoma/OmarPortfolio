pop_subjects.png: Here, you add subjects that you have and intend to study for. You need to add them here to access them later on.
pop_subjectselection.png: This is the pop-up shown after starting a session, where you select the subject you are studying.
pop_endsession.png: At the end of your studty session (following the pomodoro timer), you can either start another study cycle or end the session.
pop_enddetails.png: If you end the session, you will be prompted to enter what you studied and additional notes.
pop_studyhistory.png: A view for all of your subjects and their associated study sessions (which display cycles completed and sections studied).
